## Create the app

1. Open [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com)
2. My Apps → + → New App
3. Platforms: iOS
4. Name: `Ahmed`
5. Bundle ID: `com.ahmed.app` (create it first in Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles)
6. User access: Full Access for Ahmed Al-Saadi
